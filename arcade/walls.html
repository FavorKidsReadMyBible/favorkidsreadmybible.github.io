Guard the wall!

Nehemiah and his friends guarded the walls of Jerusalem. Now it’s your turn! Balloons are floating toward the city gate. Build towers to pop them before they get through.

Tap a tower below, then tap the grass to build it. Tap a tower on the map to upgrade it.

Balloons have layers: each pop peels one off. Red → blue → green → gold → pink. Pink balloons are the toughest!
