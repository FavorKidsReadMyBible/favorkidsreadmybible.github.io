Flock.io

You’re a shepherd! Gather lost sheep and your flock grows behind you. Reach the sheep goal to win the level. Don’t walk into another flock or the fence. Get them to walk into yours!

Move: put your thumb anywhere and slide it like a joystick (or use the mouse / arrow keys). Hold RUN (or Space) to go faster, but you’ll drop a sheep.
